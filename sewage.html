SEWAGE TREATMENT SOLUTIONS

Treat wastewater. Protect our environment.

Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) are designed to treat wastewater generated by homes, apartments, commercial buildings and other facilities before it is discharged or reused for suitable purposes.

A well-planned treatment system combines physical, biological and, where required, advanced treatment processes to help reduce contaminants and improve treated-water quality.

The appropriate plant design depends on wastewater characteristics, daily flow, available space and the intended use of the treated water.

THE TREATMENT PROCESS

A systematic approach to wastewater treatment.

Treatment stages are selected according to the plant design and wastewater requirements. Each stage has a specific role in improving water quality.

01

Preliminary Treatment

Screens and grit removal systems help remove larger debris and unwanted materials before the wastewater enters further treatment stages.

02

Primary Treatment

Settling processes help separate suspended solids from wastewater, reducing the load on subsequent treatment units.

03

Biological Treatment

Biological processes use microorganisms to break down biodegradable organic matter under controlled treatment conditions.

04

Final Treatment

Depending on the system, filtration and disinfection may be included to meet the required treated-water quality standards.
